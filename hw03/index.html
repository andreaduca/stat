Caesar vs toy EC-ElGamal

Two ciphers from two eras, one question: where does insecurity come from? Caesar adds a fixed secret shift to every letter; textbook EC-ElGamal on secp256k1 adds a fresh secret point to every message. Both are broken here — Caesar by the statistics of the language it leaks, the toy ElGamal by the small range its private key is drawn from.

Every page runs in your browser on the real curve; only the key range is toy.
