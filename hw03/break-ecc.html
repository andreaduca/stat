Breaking the toy EC-ElGamal key

The toy scheme is the real secp256k1 with one change: the private key is drawn from [1, 2bits] instead of [1, n − 1]. That single restriction is enough. From the public key alone, three searches below recover d — by trying every key, by trading memory for time, and with two hopping kangaroos — and the experiment measures how their work grows, all the way to real key sizes.
