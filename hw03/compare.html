Caesar and toy EC-ElGamal, compared

Both ciphers add a mask in a group, and both are broken on these pages — but for opposite reasons. Caesar leaks the statistics of the language and is broken by recognising English; the toy ElGamal leaks nothing and is broken by searching its small key space. This page puts the two side by side and sorts every weakness by where it comes from: the size of a key space, or the design.
