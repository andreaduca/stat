Penetration as a random walk

m attackers probe n systems one after the other, and every probe adds a small random score: 0 or 1 (Bernoulli), or −1 or +1 (Rademacher). The total is a random walk. As n grows, the average score settles on a constant (law of large numbers). The fluctuations, scaled by √n, take the same Gaussian shape whatever the step (central limit theorem). These pages simulate it, compute the exact answers next to the simulation, and ask what system size and the number of attackers mean for a defender.
